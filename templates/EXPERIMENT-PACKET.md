# Discriminating experiment

- Experiment/question/requirement IDs:
- Reference build and authorized test boundary:
- Competing hypotheses and their distinct predictions:
- First-principles invariants and current evidence:
- Primary-source research and relevant artifact locations:
- Controlled initial state, role, tenant, flags, locale/timezone and device:
- Reset method and factors held constant:
- Input/intervention and expected observable differences:
- Tool/host/provider versions and exact commands or procedure:
- Repetition, timing and nondeterminism handling:
- External-service permissions, quotas and budget:
- Raw evidence paths and redaction rules:
- Failure/confounder checks:
- Stop condition and next experiment if inconclusive:

Result: actual observations; prediction comparison; contradicted/surviving explanations; new counterexample test; remaining ambiguity; implementation choice and limits. Never label an experiment executed until raw results exist.
